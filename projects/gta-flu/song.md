# GTA Flu
Angle: the narrator calls in sick for GTA VI launch day. Thirteen years of waiting,
then the prep, then the "GTA flu". One core metaphor: the wait is an illness,
the launch is the cure. Same shape as Come Back at Five: spoken intro, hook,
verse (split into two 8-line blocks to test whether ACE-Step drops fewer lines),
hook, spoken bridge, final hook with a twist, outro that loops to the intro.
Hook rhymes ride the "oo" vowel (flu, through, new, too) because it holds when sung.
Target about two minutes.

Style: comedy trap played completely straight, dark and hard-hitting, half-time 140 bpm, booming 808s with slides, rolling triplet hi-hats, 80s Miami synth lead, deadpan male rap vocal, catchy melodic chant hook, spoken skits, ad-libs
Exclude: choir, acoustic guitar, pop-punk, happy major key
Banned:
